# Core concepts and daily rules

So how does a day on Dayli work?

The idea is simple, one post each day to reflect on what happened. You respond to the day's prompt and rate how you're feeling from 1 to 10. You can also add a caption if there's more you want to say.

## One post a day

Dayli uses the Auckland calendar day. For now, everyone follows the same day, even if you're somewhere else in the world.

You can submit one post for that day. It needs to reach Dayli and be accepted before midnight in Auckland, so leaving a draft open or writing it offline doesn't count as posting.

If you miss a day, you can't go back and submit a post for it. You can still write a new post for the current day.

## Sharing with friends or keeping it private

Before posting, you choose who the post is for.

- **Friends** means your friends can see it once it is released.
- **Solo** means the post stays private to you.

You don't have to share every day with other people. Sometimes you might just want to write things down for yourself.

## Why posts wait until midnight

Friends posts are released at the next Auckland midnight. Until then, you can see your own post, but your friends can't.

This gives everyone the day to write their own reflection before catching up on each other's days.

For example, if you post on Monday at 8 pm in Auckland, your friends can see it from midnight when Tuesday starts. Their Tuesday feed shows Monday's posts. Older shared posts can still be found on your profile.
