Period: 21 to 27 September 2026 (Pacific/Auckland).

We merged our first application foundations this week. Our work covered authentication (Anton), the posting model and composers (Jos), and private media reservations (JooHui).

## Our progress

- We merged Anton's Better Auth proof in [PR #71](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/71), followed by the Supabase-to-Neon boundary ([PR #82](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/82)), PostgreSQL persistence ([PR #83](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/83)), user/login migrations ([PR #84](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/84)), and Google sign-in and auth emails ([PR #85](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/85)).
- We added private R2 uploads and media reservations (JooHui) in [PR #86](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/86) for [#21](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/issues/21).
- We built Auckland posting-day rules, prompts, post storage, relationships, and permission filters (Jos). We merged these foundations in [PR #97](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/97), [PR #98](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/98), [PR #108](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/108), [PR #111](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/111), [PR #112](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/112), and [PR #109](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/109).
- We temporarily reverted our stack integration in [PR #94](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/94) after [PR #93](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/93). We then merged the individual foundations through separate PRs.
- We added idempotent post creation and the web composer (Jos) in [PR #113](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/113), then the Flutter shell, protected draft storage, and composer in [PR #114](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/114).
- We consolidated local auth setup (Anton) in [PR #115](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/115) and prepared staging deployment and authentication configuration in [PR #117](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/117), [PR #118](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/118), and [PR #122](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/122).

## Next steps

We still needed to connect our Flutter composer to post submission, complete upload validation, and build our friends feed and post detail views.
