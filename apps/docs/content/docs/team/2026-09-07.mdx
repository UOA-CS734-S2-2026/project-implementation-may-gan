Period: 7 to 13 September 2026 (Pacific/Auckland).

We established our monorepo and agreed on our architecture and product policies before building the application. In Anton's architecture proposal ([PR #3](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/3)), we chose Flutter and Next.js clients, a Hono API on Cloudflare Workers, PostgreSQL with Drizzle, Better Auth, and private R2 media storage.

## Our progress

- We merged Jos's contribution guidelines and issue/PR templates in [PR #2](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/2). We also initialised our monorepo (Anton) in [PR #4](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/4).
- We recorded our agreed product policies (Anton) in [PR #5](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/5). Our policies cover friendship history, released edits, blocking, public links, media limits, retention, and recovery targets.
- We defined REST and OpenAPI contracts (Anton) in [PR #52](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/52), added API documentation in [PR #55](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/55), and generated TypeScript and Dart clients in [PR #56](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/56).
- We configured Drizzle connectivity (Jos) in [PR #57](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/57). We verified staging Hyperdrive connectivity (Anton) in [PR #59](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/pull/59).
- We opened our initial task backlog, covering accounts, posting, media, messaging, reflection, sharing, and verification.

## Our decisions

We adopted our architecture proposal and [product decisions](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/blob/main/docs/dayli/product-decisions.md) as our implementation plan.

## Next steps

We continued our database migrations and transaction checks under [#8](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/issues/8) and [#9](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/issues/9). We continued tracking authentication compatibility in [#10](https://github.com/UOA-CS734-S2-2026/project-implementation-may-gan/issues/10).
