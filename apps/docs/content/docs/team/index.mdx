The team held meetings but did not keep dated minutes. The weekly records below reconstruct project progress from GitHub issues, pull requests, and main-branch commits through 4 October 2026. They cannot confirm meeting dates, attendance, spoken decisions, or agreed deadlines.

Use the linked PRs and issues to check what changed and who worked on it. Weeks follow Monday to Sunday in Pacific/Auckland. A PR appears as delivered work in the week it merged. Issue status and assignees reflect the history snapshot, because current assignment does not establish who owned an issue in an earlier week.

## Task breakdown and future meetings

The [task breakdown](/docs/team/task-breakdown) lists the recorded contributions and the issue backlog with its current assignments. The [meeting template](/docs/team/meeting-template) is ready for future meetings: record the actual date, attendance, decisions, and issue-linked actions during the meeting, then open a PR for the notes.

A weekly progress record helps explain the repository history. The course requirement for weekly meeting minutes still needs dated meeting notes going forward.
