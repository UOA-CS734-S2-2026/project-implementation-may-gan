# Friends and the feed

Dayli waits until the next Auckland day before showing a Friends post to friends. The feed is a catch-up on yesterday, not a live view of what people are writing today.

## Find someone by username

On the web, use the username search in the navigation. On mobile:

1. Tap **friends** in the bottom bar.
2. Stay on the **Friends** tab.
3. Tap **Find people by username**.
4. Search for the exact person you want, then open their profile.

From a web profile, you can add the person, accept a request they have already sent, see when your request is pending, or remove an existing friend. You can also start a message from the profile.

On a mobile profile, a pending request may only say that a request is waiting or was sent. Use the **Requests** tab to act on it.

## Handle friend requests

Open **friends**, then choose **Requests**.

- Under **Received**, choose **Accept** or **Decline**.
- Under **Sent**, choose **Cancel** if you no longer want to send the request.
- In your friends list, choose **Remove friend** to end an existing friendship.

Only an active friendship gives someone access to your Friends posts.

## What appears in daylies

Open **daylies** from the web navigation or mobile bottom bar. The feed shows Friends posts from active friends for yesterday's Auckland posting day, after those posts have been released.

It does not show:

- a friend's Solo posts
- a friend's post before its release time
- your own posts
- posts from someone who is not currently your friend

Use **my days** to see your own posts. Open a friend's profile to browse their older released Friends posts that you can still access.

If a post says **This dayli isn't available. It may have been deleted, or you may no longer have access.**, refresh the page. If it remains unavailable, your relationship or access may have changed.

## Messages and requests

Open **messages** on web or mobile. Conversations are split into **Messages** and **Requests**.

Friends can start an active conversation straight away. Someone who is not your friend can send one initial message request. The recipient must accept it before the conversation becomes active. They can also decline it.

Messages are plain text and can contain up to 4,000 characters. In an active conversation you can reply, react, and see read receipts. You can edit a message for 15 minutes after sending it, or unsend it. Editing a message does not change any dayli post.

Dayli messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Dayli's authorized backend can read stored message content to provide the service. Unsend cannot take back text that somebody has already seen, copied, or captured.
